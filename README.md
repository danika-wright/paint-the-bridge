# Paint the Bridge

A five-minute curriculum renewal game for MBA and executive education leaders. Choose the two
capabilities your program will protect, then mark seven decisions twice: what your school does
today, and what you would want. The condition report shows where the gaps are, and the three
biggest become moves for Monday.

Play it: https://danika-wright.github.io/paint-the-bridge/

A companion to the talk *Degrees of freedom: Preparing graduates to lead what comes next* at the
AMBA & BGA Asia Pacific Deans and Directors Conference, Singapore, November 2026. The scores are a
conversation starter, not a measurement. Sources are listed in the app.

The page is a single HTML file. It stores nothing and sends nothing; fonts load from Google Fonts.
